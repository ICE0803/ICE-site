          Description

          :           "Meet Yourself" is a pastoral healing drama directed by Ding Ziguang, starring Liu Yifei and Li Xian, with special appearances by Hu Bingqing and Niu Junfeng, and featuring Wu Yanshu, Dong Qing, Fan Shuaqi, Ma Mengwei, Zhao Ziqi, and special guest appearances by Tu Songyan, Liu Jia, Hao Ping, Gong Beibi, and Yao Anna. Wu Qian, Zeng Shunxi, Shi Pengyuan, and Liu Meihan also make friendly guest appearances. The drama follows Xu Hongdou, who moves to Yunnan for a short stay after resigning from her job. There, she unexpectedly meets Xie Zhiyiao, who gave up a high-paying career to return home and start a business. After moving into the breezy Xiaoyuan residence, Xu Hongdou encounters tenants with diverse backgrounds and stories, weaving a heartwarming tale of connection, growth, and healing.
